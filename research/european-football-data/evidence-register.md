## Claim-appropriate evidence rule

There is no universal evidence hierarchy. For **actual data contents**,
direct API/repository observations are the relevant evidence, with
sample scope stated. For **permissions and restrictions**, the governing
published terms/licence are the relevant evidence; technical capability
does not establish legal permission. For **advertised capabilities,
identifiers, packages and coverage mechanisms**, current official
documentation/coverage/pricing pages are the relevant evidence; examples
remain examples. Independent review is an additional review-status
attribute, not a substitute for the correct source type.
