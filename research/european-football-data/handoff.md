## Evidence rule corrected

Evidence is claim-specific rather than universally ranked. Raw/API
observations support statements about actual contents. Published
terms/licences support permissions and restrictions. Current official
documentation supports advertised capabilities, identifiers, packages
and coverage mechanisms. A same-researcher recheck increases
traceability but is not independent review.
