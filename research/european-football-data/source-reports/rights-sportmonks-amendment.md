## SM-RIGHTS-12 — VAT and currency

**Source:** Sportmonks Terms of Service  
**Sections:** **VAT**; **Currency**  
**Supporting excerpts:** “All prices are exclusive of VAT”; “All payments are processed in Euros (€).”

**Finding:** The general published Terms state that Sportmonks prices exclude VAT. VAT is calculated based on country and, where applicable, charged at the standard rate. The Terms also state that a valid VAT number can be used to exempt taxation charges. Payments are processed in euros; other currencies are converted to euros by the billing system at the current exchange rate.

**Limit:** This is Sportmonks' general contractual billing statement, not tax advice or a determination of the VAT treatment applicable to any particular customer or transaction.

The original rights classifications remain otherwise unchanged.
