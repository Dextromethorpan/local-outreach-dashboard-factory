## Claim register

### SM-RIGHTS-01 — Commercial creations based on numerical data
**Source:** Sportmonks Terms of Service, “Terms of use,” lines 125–138 in the inspected page.  
**Finding:** Sportmonks expressly contemplates apps, websites and games built from its data and says monetizing a creation based on the data is generally acceptable, while direct data resale is not.  
**Classification:** Explicitly permitted subject to anti-resale boundary.

### SM-RIGHTS-02 — Distribution, transfer and storage
**Source:** Sportmonks Terms of Service, “Copyright,” lines 139–144.  
**Finding:** The Terms distinguish the protected Sportmonks service from “data provided by our services,” expressly allowing distribution, transfer and storage of the latter while prohibiting resale without consent.  
**Classification:** Explicit permission with resale restriction.

### SM-RIGHTS-03 — Raw resale restriction
**Source:** Sportmonks Terms of Service, “Terms of use,” lines 135–138; “Copyright,” lines 141–144.  
**Finding:** Direct resale of Sportmonks data without approval/consent is prohibited.  
**Classification:** Explicitly restricted unless approved.

### SM-RIGHTS-04 — Derived analysis
**Source:** Sportmonks Terms of Service, lines 135–136; Enterprise permitted-use explanation, lines 227–250.  
**Finding:** Creating and monetizing something based on the data is permitted in principle; the Enterprise explanation confirms building commercial products and showing data to users.  
**Classification:** Permitted in principle.  
**Limit:** No formal definition of derived data or transformation threshold was found.

### SM-RIGHTS-05 — Caching/storage
**Source:** Terms, Copyright lines 141–144; Enterprise explanation lines 227–250.  
**Finding:** Storage is expressly permitted; the subordinate Enterprise explanation expressly mentions caching in the customer's own database.  
**Classification:** Explicitly permitted while subscribed/using the service.  
**Limit:** No retention period or post-termination rule found.

### SM-RIGHTS-06 — Attribution
**Source:** Full inspected Terms, especially Terms of use/Copyright and Logo/image rights.  
**Finding:** No general attribution requirement for numerical API data was found. Attribution appears in the logo/photo discussion as one suggested IP-compliance step.  
**Classification:** Numerical-data attribution: not found. Media attribution: not an independent substitute for obtaining necessary rights.

### SM-RIGHTS-07 — Logo and profile-photo rights
**Source:** Terms, “Logo/image rights,” lines 160–179.  
**Finding:** Sportmonks states that logos/profile photos are copyrighted by their legal owners and that the API customer must arrange proof of the right/permission to use them.  
**Classification:** No Sportmonks media-rights grant established.

### SM-RIGHTS-08 — Video
**Source:** Terms, “Video rights,” lines 180–182.  
**Finding:** Video highlights are embedded via third-party video URLs and use is at the customer's risk.  
**Classification:** No affirmative reproduction/redistribution licence found.

### SM-RIGHTS-09 — Cancellation and termination
**Source:** Terms, Cancellation & refund policy lines 91–108; General Terms lines 148–156.  
**Finding:** Customer cancellation normally leaves access active through the paid billing period; Sportmonks may terminate immediately for a direct Terms violation.  
**Classification:** Explicit.  
**Limit:** No clause found governing retention/use of previously stored data after access ends.

### SM-RIGHTS-10 — Website/service materials versus API data
**Source:** Terms, Copyright lines 139–144.  
**Finding:** Sportmonks website materials are subject to a much narrower personal/non-commercial rule, while the Terms separately permit distribution/transfer/storage of data provided by the services.  
**Classification:** Distinct rights categories; website-media restrictions should not be mechanically applied to numerical API data, nor vice versa.

### SM-RIGHTS-11 — Official competition rights caveat
**Source:** Sportmonks Enterprise Plan, “Official rights, stated plainly,” lines 245–250 and FAQ lines 375–379.  
**Finding:** Sportmonks states it is a data provider rather than an official rights holder for the competitions and says officially licensed data, where required, is a separate arrangement.  
**Classification:** Provider disclosure; no independent legal conclusion drawn.

## Material unresolved questions

1. **Post-termination data:** May previously stored numerical data and derived outputs continue to be retained, displayed, republished or delivered after the subscription terminates?
2. **Customer publication/export boundary:** At what point do downloadable/exported numerical outputs, reports, customer publications or bulk extracts become prohibited resale/redistribution?
3. **Derived datasets:** Are transformed/aggregated datasets freely distributable where reconstruction of the underlying feed is impossible, and what test does Sportmonks use?
4. **Multiple domains:** What constitutes a separate domain for pricing/licensing purposes, including customer-controlled domains or white-label deployments?
5. **Third-party/add-on terms:** Do Premium Odds, news, transfer rumours or other partner-sourced feeds carry additional redistribution, attribution, retention or territory restrictions?
6. **Media:** Sportmonks does not supply the required logo/photo IP permission under the standard Terms; any intended media use needs separate rights evidence.
7. **Custom/Enterprise terms:** A negotiated agreement can extend the standard position. No such agreement was inspected.

These are unresolved contractual questions only; no vendor contact is authorized by this assignment.
