## Do not collapse these distinctions

Coordination is an umbrella, not a single validated product problem. Cross-system schedule reconciliation has one retained account (E07). Onboarding trouble (E08) and nonresponse despite an app (E09) have different causes. E10 and E11 show that adopting another platform is not an established remedy.

Fan discovery and subscription entitlement are different. An information service might help someone locate a game, but cannot grant missing broadcast rights or make subscriptions affordable. Replay behavior is another separate activity.

Creator footage uncertainty does not establish that numerical match statistics are required. Beginner interest is not an established publishing business. Team coaches are an emergent segment, not a replacement for the original creator audience without a deliberate scope decision.

## Deduplication and uncertainty

Each thread remains one source cluster regardless of comments. E09's public author also replies in P13; that reply is not counted as another supporting person. Repeated TeamSnap comments in P13 were not counted twice. Deleted/promotional OP content in P01/P02 and vendor replies elsewhere were excluded. Identities across platforms were not investigated.

Relative dates were retained as uncertain rather than converted into exact dates. E14/E15 are outside the preferred 24-month window. Satisfaction accounts differ in role, league and service setup; they challenge universal claims, not every specific incident.
