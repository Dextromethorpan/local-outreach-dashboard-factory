## Confirmed research scope

One technical test only: English Championship, current season required and previous seasons optional. The proposed output is a static fixture/result/standings graphic exported as PNG or SVG for an independent creator. Mandatory data are competition and season, stable match and team identifiers, kickoff timestamp with explicit timezone, home/away teams, match status, completed-match score, and standings position/team/played/won/drawn/lost/goal difference/points. Crests, logos, other third-party images, other competitions, live data, advanced events and xG are excluded. Six hours after match completion is a provisional maximum to test, not a provider guarantee. Budget and production scale are unknown.

## Status vocabulary

- **Unresolved:** evidence needed for the scoped decision is absent, contradictory or requires an authorized observation/vendor answer.
