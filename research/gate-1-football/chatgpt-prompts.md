Date: 2026-09-17. Attach report.md from this folder to the first prompt.
Run prompts sequentially. In a fresh chat, attach the report and accepted outputs
from earlier steps. Copy the shared instructions followed by ONE numbered prompt.
These prompts are assignments, not completed research or gate approvals.

## Shared instructions — include with every assignment

```text
You are researching Football Gate 1 for a proposed product serving independent
content creators, newsletter authors and small commentators. The attached report
is context, not authoritative evidence. Football is Needs Evidence. No creator
collection, outreach or gate advancement is authorized.

Use current original provider documentation and genuinely open/licensed data.
Do not scrape platforms as a product data source. Browse the cited pages directly;
if browsing is unavailable, say so and stop factual verification. Never invent
quotes, prices, endpoint paths, sample responses or legal clauses. Distinguish
facts, hypotheses, inferences, missing information and failed retrievals.

For each accepted claim provide a stable claim ID, direct URL, retrieval date,
page title/section (PDF page when applicable), a short exact supporting excerpt
or permitted sample, and the precise conclusion supported. Respect quotation and
licensing limits. Search snippets and another AI's response are leads, not proof.
Do not infer access rights from price, attribution, API availability or 'open' branding.

Spend at most one focused research pass on this assignment: inspect up to eight
primary pages, and make at most one alternate retrieval attempt per failed page.
Then stop. Return new evidence, corrected/rejected claims, and blockers with their
next action and owner (researcher, user or vendor). If nothing is resolved, say
'No new evidence; blocked.' Do not produce another broad feasibility essay.
Do not purchase access, contact anyone, expose credentials or approve gates.
```

## Prompt 1 — Define a testable provisional scope

```text
Using the attached report, propose one small football product scope for Gate 1
testing. Prefer Option A (fixtures/results/standings graphics) as a provisional
research hypothesis, not an approved business or a validated pain point. Explain
briefly if evidence warrants considering Option B instead.

Specify: user, one concrete output, proposed initial competition(s), minimum
required fields versus optional fields, historical depth, acceptable update delay,
expected request volume assumptions, and output/publication workflow. Distinguish
our API access from our customers publishing exported PNG/SVG graphics in monetized
newsletters or social posts. Creator residence is not a league-coverage requirement.
Do not silently impose worldwide coverage, lower divisions, live data or xG.

Return a one-page brief with an assumption/decision table. Ask no more than three
material questions for me to settle scope. Mark budget and scale unknown if not
supplied. Stop and wait for my scope confirmation before supplier verification.
```

## Prompt 3 — Resolve commercial publishing rights

```text
Use the confirmed brief and supplier evidence from Prompt 2. Find the selected
provider's actual current legal terms/licence, following official navigation if
an earlier /terms URL fails. Record document version/date and applicable plan.

Check separately: our commercial API use; caching/retention; transformations into
charts; exporting images; our customers republishing in monetized media; attribution;
logos/third-party assets if used; sublicensing/redistribution; termination obligations.
For each right, give the exact relevant section and classify explicitly permitted,
explicitly restricted, ambiguous or not found. Do not treat silence as permission,
or claim a document omits a clause when you could not inspect it.

If essential rights remain unclear, draft a short vendor inquiry describing our
actual workflow and requesting written confirmation plus applicable contract clauses
and package costs. Draft only; do not send. If terms cannot be retrieved, identify
one concrete human retrieval action and stop. Deliver a rights matrix and blockers,
not a legal assurance or Gate 1 approval.
```
